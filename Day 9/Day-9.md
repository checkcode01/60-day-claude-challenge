Key Learnings
1. MVP First Approach
Instead of asking Claude to build everything at once, starting with a simple version improved reliability.
2. Iterative Development Works Better
Each enhancement prompt improved quality without breaking existing functionality.
3. Prompt Engineering Matters
Clear requirements produced significantly better outputs.
4. AI Can Build Real Applications
Claude Artifacts can generate complete interactive applications, not just text.
5. Product Thinking Is Essential
Building software is about:

Challenges Faced
Challenge 1
Large prompts caused incomplete outputs.
Solution: Used MVP-first strategy.
Challenge 2
Charts became cluttered.
Solution: Enhanced visualizations incrementally.
Challenge 3
Nutrition recommendations lacked context.
Solution: Added dietary preference logic and risk analysis.
Final Observation
The biggest insight from Day 9 was:
Professional AI builders use iterative development. A working MVP delivered quickly is more valuable than a perfect product that never gets completed.
