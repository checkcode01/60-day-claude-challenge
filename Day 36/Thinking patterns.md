# Day 36 – Cognitive Pattern Explorer

## Project Overview

Built **Cognitive Pattern Explorer**, an interactive psychology-inspired self-reflection web application using Claude AI.

The application helps users explore their natural thinking tendencies through interactive scenarios, drag-and-drop exercises, and reflective journaling. It is designed for educational purposes only and does not provide any clinical assessment.

---

## Features

- Calm Mode and Stress Mode
- Interactive decision-making scenarios
- Drag-and-drop prioritization
- Thinking timeline builder
- Personalized Reflection Journal
- Thinking Style Percentage Breakdown
- Progress tracking
- Responsive interface
- Offline HTML application
- Smooth animations
- Keyboard accessibility

---

## Thinking Tendencies Explored

- Analytical Thinker
- Emotional Intuitive
- Overthinking Loop Style
- Action-First Decision Maker
- Balanced Reflective Thinker

---

## Chapters Completed

### Chapter 1 – Discover Your Thinking Style

Explored everyday situations to understand natural decision-making tendencies.

### Chapter 2 – Choose Your Priorities

Completed drag-and-drop prioritization activities to identify what influences decisions most.

### Chapter 3 – Map Your Thinking

Organized decision sequences using an interactive timeline to understand personal thought patterns.

### Final Reflection Journal

Reviewed personalized insights with percentage-based thinking tendencies and recommendations for self-reflection.

---

## Key Learnings

- Decision making changes depending on context.
- Different thinking styles have unique strengths.
- Reflection improves awareness before making important decisions.
- There is rarely one correct thinking style.
- Balanced thinking often combines logic and intuition.
