Today I built my personal portfolio website using Claude Artifacts as part of the ABTalks 60-Day Claude Challenge.

Key Learnings

1. Personal Branding Matters

A portfolio is more than a website. It is a professional story that showcases skills, achievements, and aspirations.

2. AI Accelerates Development

Claude can generate production-ready websites in minutes when provided with clear instructions and context.

3. Recruiter Experience is Important

A clean design, structured content, and clear project descriptions help recruiters quickly understand a candidate's value.

4. Showcasing Projects Creates Credibility

Practical projects demonstrate applied knowledge better than simply listing skills.

5. Consistency Builds Trust

Combining GitHub, LinkedIn, and portfolio projects creates a stronger professional presence.
