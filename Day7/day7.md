Day 7 – Claude Model Selection & Reasoning Effort

Challenge Overview

Today I learned how to select the right Claude model and reasoning effort level based on the complexity of a task.

Choosing the correct model is often more important than writing the perfect prompt because different Claude models are optimized for different workloads.

- Business Strategy & Planning
- Research & Learning
- Content Creation & Writing

Claude Usage:

- Heavy User (3+ hours daily)

My Claude Usage Strategy

Recommended Primary Model

Claude Sonnet 4.6

Sonnet provides the best balance between:

- Intelligence

It is suitable for approximately 65–70% of my daily work.

---

When to Use Haiku

Best for:

- Quick lookups
- Email drafting
- Formatting content
- Summarization
- Routine questions

Benefits:

- Fastest response speed
- Lowest resource consumption

Avoid:

- Strategic planning
- Executive reports
- Complex reasoning

---

When to Use Sonnet

Best for:

- HR content creation
- LinkedIn posts
- Research summaries
- Policy writing
- Learning new concepts
- Framework development

Benefits:

- Strong reasoning
- Fast iteration
- Excellent balance of quality and speed

Recommended Usage:

Approximately 65% of daily work.

---

When to Use Opus

Best for:

- Executive presentations
- Boardroom strategy
- Workforce planning
- Business case development
- Advanced research
- Multi-step problem solving

Benefits:

- Deepest reasoning capability
- Strong strategic thinking

Avoid:

- Simple tasks
- Routine formatting work

Recommended Usage:

Approximately 20% of daily work.

---

Recommended Effort Levels

Low

Use for:

- Simple questions
- Basic formatting
- Quick brainstorming

Example:

"Give me 5 LinkedIn headline ideas."

---

Standard

Use for:

- Daily work
- Email writing
- Summaries
- Learning support

Example:

"Write an HR policy draft."

---

High

Use for:

- Frameworks
- Reports
- Strategic analysis
- Professional deliverables

Example:

"Create a 90-day AI adoption roadmap."

---

Max

Use for:

- Board presentations
- Executive strategy documents
- High-impact leadership communication

Example:

"Build an AI Workforce Readiness Framework."

Personalized Workflow

Task| Best Model| Effort
Email Drafting| Haiku| Low
HR Queries| Haiku| Standard
LinkedIn Posts| Sonnet| Standard
Research Summaries| Sonnet| High
HR Frameworks| Sonnet| High
Business Cases| Opus| High
Board Presentations| Opus| Max
Strategic Roadmaps| Opus| Max

---

Biggest Learnings

1. Not every task requires the most powerful model.
2. Sonnet is the best daily-use model.
3. Opus should be reserved for high-value strategic work.
4. High reasoning effort improves quality significantly.
5. Max effort should be used selectively.
6. Matching model capability to task complexity improves productivity and efficiency.

---

Final Recommendation

If I could use only one model and one effort level:

Model: Claude Sonnet 4.6

Effort: High

Reason:

It provides the best balance of speed, intelligence, reasoning quality, and resource efficiency for professional users working on strategy, research, and content creation.
