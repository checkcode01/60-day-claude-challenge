Day 21 – Privacy Intelligence with Claude

Objective

Build a Digital Privacy Intelligence Dashboard that visualizes digital footprint, privacy exposure, tracking risks, company exposure, and privacy improvement opportunities using Claude.

---

What I Learned

1. Digital Footprint Analysis

I learned how different apps collectively contribute to my online presence and how a small set of services can create a large digital footprint.

2. Ecosystem Concentration

One of the most surprising findings was that a few companies such as Google, Meta, ByteDance, Amazon, and Tencent can potentially collect data across multiple services and touchpoints.

3. Digital Twin Modeling

The dashboard demonstrated how companies may estimate interests, demographics, shopping habits, entertainment preferences, and behavioral patterns based on service usage.

Important Note:
These are estimates only and not facts.

4. Exposure Heatmap

The heatmap helped visualize which platforms have access to:

- Communication data
- Location data
- Shopping behavior
- Payment activity
- Search history
- Photos and media
- Entertainment preferences

5. Risk Assessment

The dashboard highlighted:

- Cross-platform tracking risks
- Ecosystem lock-in
- Advertising profile creation
- Behavioral prediction risks
- Data aggregation risks

6. Privacy Improvement Planning

The simulator showed how privacy scores can improve by:

- Enabling MFA
- Reducing unused apps
- Limiting permissions
- Reviewing account privacy settings
- Diversifying ecosystems

---

Key Insights

1. A small number of platforms can accumulate large amounts of user data.
2. Cross-platform ecosystems increase convenience but also increase exposure.
3. Privacy risk is not only about security breaches but also about data aggregation.
4. Regular privacy reviews can significantly improve digital safety.
5. Understanding digital footprints helps make informed technology decisions.
